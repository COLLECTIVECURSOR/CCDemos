## Targeted live research refresh — 2026-08-24

- The freshly loaded Pins Database showed 151,573 active results, 40 records on page one, 3,790 pages, Oldest/Newest controls, and recent identifiers through PP191156. A PP identifier is treated as a record identifier, not as the database-result total.
- The current Forums expose Introductions & Help, Pinformation & Games, Disney Pin Discussion, Pin ID Help, Selling, Fantasy Disney Pins, Announcements, recent posts, online users, and topic creation.
- The live Events area exposes Create Event, Subscribe, Search Events, Online Events, month browsing, event dates/times, descriptions, and individual event pages.
- The current FAQs document free registration and Trade Assist, database-first searching, Pin ID Help, approval-based submissions, and the current eligibility requirement of 30 days of membership plus three forum posts before submitting a new listing.
- A current pin record exposes PP number/title, Pin Description, Origins, Edition, Year of Release, Original Price, SKU, Dimensions, AP/PP availability, Owns/Wants/Trades counts, and linked pins. Private notes/storage/value remain Plus/Premium features.
- The live registration page currently requests display name, email, password, country, location, collections, first and last name, news preference, and Terms agreement. No local account is created by this concept.
- Current advertised prices were verified as Premium Annual $168/year, Premium Monthly $15.99/month, and Plus $5.99/month. They remain omitted from the local Membership page so the concept does not present a static price as continuously current.
- The official homepage currently links “Announcing the Submit-a-Pin Weekend!” at `https://pinpics.com/forums/topic/4468-announcing-the-submit-a-pin-weekend/`; the Discover editorial board now links to that verified source directly.
